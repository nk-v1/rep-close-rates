Rep Close Rates

How to use this

  • Overall shows each rep's close rate across all cycles. Active reps are on top, former reps below, each from highest close rate to lowest.
  • Pick a cycle tab (or click a bar in the timeline) to see that cycle's reps.
  • Org level shows the whole team per cycle: leads, closes, conversion and refunds, then closes by month and refund timing.
  • By rep shows one rep cycle by cycle. Pick from Active rep, or from Former rep below it.
  • Leads = leads the rep owned that cycle. Touched = leads with any CRM status (not blank). Consumed = leads with a status other than DNP, No Response or blank. Still DNP = leads whose status is DNP today.
  • Close rate = closes ÷ leads. Closes come from the payment sheets.
  • A dash or "no rate" means that cycle has missing lead data (C4, C5 and part of C8). C9 is still open.
